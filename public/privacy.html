Privacy Policy — Loom Storyteller

Last updated: October 9, 2026

This policy covers the Loom Storyteller apps on iPhone and iPad, the web app at loom.ibm.io, and related open-data catalog proxies. Contact: mhsenkow@gmail.com.

Summary

Loom is local-first. We do not require an account, and we do not run analytics, advertising, or user tracking SDKs in the App Store build. Data you load stays on your device unless you choose a live/open-data feed that fetches over the network.

Data we do not collect

  • No account, email, or profile signup
  • No advertising identifiers or cross-app tracking
  • No crash/analytics SDKs that upload usage histories to us
  • Local CSV / Files you open stay on the device

Network activity you may choose

When you open a live or open-data source (for example earthquakes, flights, Wikipedia edits, weather, or a catalog listing), the app requests that public data over HTTPS from the upstream provider and/or our Cloudflare Worker proxy. Those requests are made to show you the chart or listing you asked for. We do not sell that activity and we do not use it to build advertising profiles.

Our Cloudflare Workers may log request metadata (URL path, status, timing) for abuse prevention and operations. We do not intentionally log chart data or uploaded file contents in those logs. MCP demo chat should not put your dataset in URL query parameters (see product privacy review LOOM-506).

Shared chart links (when you use share-to-link) store the chart configuration needed to reopen the view. Do not put secrets in shared titles or fields.

Third-party content

Live feeds and open-data catalogs display content from third-party public sources (for example USGS, Wikipedia, Open-Meteo, and similar providers). Those providers have their own terms and privacy practices. Loom does not claim ownership of that upstream data.

Children

Loom Storyteller is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this page when the product changes. The “Last updated” date above will change when we do.
